COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know before we start.

Here are a few things businesses commonly ask about working with Syntora.

Does the package include advertising spend?

Not automatically. Service fees and platform advertising spend are separate unless your approved proposal clearly says otherwise. We will confirm the budget before a campaign starts.

Which platforms can you support?

Our core focus is Facebook, Instagram and TikTok ads. We can also discuss YouTube, LinkedIn and WhatsApp based on your audience, content and campaign objective.

Can you guarantee followers or sales?

No responsible campaign can guarantee a specific number of followers, enquiries or sales. We focus on sound planning, relevant targeting, useful creative and transparent performance reviews.

What do you need from my business?

Usually your business details, campaign objective, target audience, offer, brand assets and access or permissions needed for the agreed work. We will guide you through the requirements.

Can I request a custom package?

Yes. Share your goals, timeline and estimated budget through the enquiry form and we can discuss a scope that fits your needs.

How do I get started?

Choose a package or submit the enquiry form below. It will prepare a message for WhatsApp so you can confirm the details directly with our team.
